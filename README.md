High School Student • Indie Software Developer • Builder
--------------------------------------------------------

I’m Kenta, a high school student and indie software developer based in Bangkok, Thailand. I’m passionate about software development, artificial intelligence, technology, electronics, and building things that turn ideas into real, usable products.

A lot of what I know comes from learning by doing. Rather than only studying programming concepts, I like taking an idea, designing it, building it, breaking it, improving it, and eventually turning it into something people can actually use. I work on independent software projects where I handle everything from the initial concept and UI/UX design to development, testing, deployment, documentation, and ongoing updates.

One of my main projects is StageWrite AI, a music-writing and production tool designed to make songwriting, music theory, and composition more accessible and practical. It combines two of my biggest interests software and music and has given me experience developing a larger project over time rather than treating it as a one-off experiment. I’m constantly adding features, redesigning parts of the experience, fixing problems, and exploring ways AI can make creative software more useful.

Outside of programming, I’m heavily involved in music and technical production. I play guitar, compose music, experiment with DAWs and MIDI, and work on theatre productions at school. As a Tech Director, I’ve worked with lighting, audio, QLab, visual backdrops, cue systems, and production planning. I enjoy the technical side of live events because it combines technology with creativity while requiring everything to work reliably when it matters.

I’m also interested in AI, computer science, electronics, engineering, and the technology behind the products we use every day. I like experimenting with new tools and using AI as part of my development workflow not just to generate code, but to help prototype ideas, solve problems, research unfamiliar concepts, and iterate faster.

I’m still early in my journey, and there is a huge amount I want to learn. My goal is to keep building increasingly ambitious projects, improve as a software developer, contribute to projects with real users, and develop a portfolio that reflects what I can actually create rather than just what I have studied.

Long term, I’m interested in pursuing software engineering and working on technology that solves real problems. For now, I’m focused on learning, experimenting, shipping projects, and getting better with every version I build.
